# Business Insights

Run the SQL scripts against the supplied PostgreSQL dataset and record the actual results here.

## Recommended management questions

- Which categories drive the most revenue and gross profit?
- Which regions are growing fastest?
- How strong is customer repeat purchasing?
- Which products contribute disproportionately to revenue?
- Which high-value customers may need retention activity?
- Which products have strong revenue but weak margins?

## Recommendations framework

1. Protect high-margin products with strong demand.
2. Target high-value repeat customers with loyalty campaigns.
3. Investigate regions with declining month-over-month revenue.
4. Review discounting where discounts reduce gross margin without improving volume.
5. Use product-level rankings to prioritise inventory and marketing.
